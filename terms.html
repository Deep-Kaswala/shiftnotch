What this app is

Shiftnotch records the shifts you enter and estimates what they're worth from the rates you enter. It is a personal record, not a payroll system and not legal or financial advice.

Check your pay yourself

The totals are only as right as what you type in, and rules like penalty rates, overtime, allowances and tax are not fully modelled. Always check against your payslip and your employer's own records before relying on a figure.

Your own copy

Shifts are stored on your device. Keeping a backup is up to you — export a PDF, or subscribe to Pro and they're stored for your account too. We can't recover data from a phone that's been lost or wiped.

Pro

Pro is a subscription billed by Apple. It renews automatically until cancelled, and you cancel or manage it in iOS Settings, not here. Payment is taken by Apple when the free trial, if any, ends.
