Shiftnotch

Know what the week is worth before payday

Shiftnotch logs a shift in two taps, works out the pay from your own rates, and exports a clean timesheet when someone asks for one.

Support

Questions, a bug, or something that looks wrong on your timesheet — email shiftnotch.app@gmail.com. Say which iPhone you're on and what you expected to see.

If a pay figure looks wrong, check the employer's rates and penalties in the Companies tab first — that is where almost every surprise comes from.

Deleting your account

Open Shiftnotch, go to Settings → Delete Account. That erases your sign-in, everything stored for it on the server, and that account's shifts on the phone. You do not need to email anyone to do it.

It does not cancel an App Store subscription — cancel that in iOS Settings under your name, then Subscriptions.
