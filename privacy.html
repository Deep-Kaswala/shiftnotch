Privacy Policy

Last updated 26 November 2026

Who we are

Shiftnotch is run from Victoria, Australia. “We” in this policy means Shiftnotch. You can reach us at support@shiftnotch.com.

What we store

If you create an account we store your email address, your name, an account id, and the version of our terms you agreed to and when. Your password is never stored, only a scrambled (hashed) form of it that can't be turned back into the password. When you sign in, our sign-in service also records your IP address and the kind of device, to keep your account secure. Nothing else about you is collected.

Your shifts

Your shifts, companies, notes and pay rates are saved on this iPhone. They are sent to our server only if you subscribe to Pro, which exists so they reach your other devices. Without Pro they never leave the phone.

Where it is kept

Accounts and Pro sync run on Supabase, whose database for Shiftnotch is in Sydney. Supabase is based in Singapore, and it and the companies it relies on to run its service, such as Amazon Web Services, may handle your information from outside Australia. Sign-in emails are sent through Resend, an email delivery service based in the United States. Apple, also based in the United States, handles payment and tells us only whether a subscription is active.

Who else sees it

No one else. There is no analytics, no tracking and no advertising, and nothing is sold. The companies named above handle your information only to run Shiftnotch for you.

Deleting it

Settings → Delete Account erases your sign-in and everything stored for it on our server, and removes that account's shifts from this iPhone. It can't be undone, and it does not cancel an App Store subscription — do that in Settings → Manage Subscription first. Exports you have shared, and Apple's own records of your purchases, are outside our control.

Keeping it

We keep what's listed above for as long as the account exists. When you delete it, it is removed from our database straight away. Short-lived copies in our hosting provider's logs and backups expire on their own within days.

Children

You must be at least 13 to use Shiftnotch, and if you're under 18 you need a parent's or guardian's permission. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, email us and we'll delete it.

Your rights and complaints

You can see and change your name in Settings, see your shifts in the app, and remove everything with Delete Account. To ask for a copy of what we hold, to correct it, or to complain about how we've handled it, email support@shiftnotch.com and we'll reply within 30 days. If you're not happy with our answer, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

Changes

If this policy changes we'll publish the new version with a new date. This version is dated 26 November 2026.
