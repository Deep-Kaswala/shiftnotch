Privacy Policy

Last updated 23 November 2026

What we store

If you create an account we store your email address and an account id, so you can sign back in. If you set a name for your timesheets we store that too. Nothing else about you is collected.

Your shifts

Your shifts, companies, notes and pay rates are saved on your iPhone. They are sent to our server only if you subscribe to Pro, which exists so they reach your other devices. Without Pro they never leave the phone.

Where it is kept

Accounts and Pro sync run on Supabase in their Sydney region (ap-southeast-2), so the data stays in Australia. Supabase is the only company that stores it; Apple handles payment and tells us nothing about you beyond whether a subscription is active.

Who else sees it

Nobody. There is no analytics, no tracking, no advertising, and nothing is sold or shared.

Deleting it

Settings → Delete Account erases your sign-in and everything stored for it on the server, and removes that account's shifts from your iPhone. It can't be undone, and it does not cancel an App Store subscription — do that in iOS Settings.

Keeping it

We keep what's listed above for as long as the account exists. Delete the account and it goes, immediately and for good. Nothing is retained in a backup for longer than 7 days.

Children

Shiftnotch is not aimed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect anything from them.

Asking us about your data

You can see everything we hold by signing in, and remove it with Delete Account. If you'd rather ask a person, email support@shiftnotch.com.
